Crew

The trio

Picture Pink Floyd and Hendrix jamming with Hilight Tribe, High Tone and Infected Mushroom… somewhere in another dimension.

Traveling between interstellar, cosmic and psychedelic sounds, these three musicians have as their common denominator emotional sharing, energy transfer and letting go!

Founded in the early 2010’s by artists from the 79 (Melle / Sainte-Soline, France) from the punk/metal, funk, reggae and street arts scenes, ChabiFönK Experience distills unique hybrid music with a “fönky” groove, fusion of rock!

“Big riffs à la RATM or Hendrix and the electro-underground of the Rave Party”

Music

EXPLÖR3

“EXPLÖR3”, ChabiFönK Experience's 3rd studio album, takes you on a cosmic auditory adventure marking ten years of the band.

This ‘concept album’ is a true musical odyssey, unburdened by conventions, boldly fusing multiple genres like rock, psychedelic, techno, trance, jungle, drum & bass, dub, krautrock, and progressive metal. « EXPLÖR3 » will transport you beyond the stratosphere, where each track is a radiant star in this unique musical galaxy.

Released on 2 November 2023 on CD, vinyl and streaming. Recorded and mixed at Studio de l'Arnoult by Alexis Lacaze, mastered at Studio Storm Mastering by Clément Bastiat. Artwork: SandFlake.
